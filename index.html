About KenyaQuickTools

Practical, lightweight tools for people building something of their own.

Simple tools, made to get the job done

KenyaQuickTools is a small independent project concept for Kenyan freelancers, creators and small businesses. The first tools help you estimate profit and prepare a basic invoice using Kenyan shillings.

The calculations run in your browser and this starter website does not connect to a database, payment service, analytics provider or advertising network. Results are estimates: check your figures and applicable business requirements before relying on them.
